## Modes

AUTO

Automatically selects an available provider.

ONLINE

Uses configured online providers.

OFFLINE

Uses local provider only.

PRIVATE

Does not intentionally send private data to online providers.

## Truthfulness

Tool results must be verified.

The AI must not claim successful execution without actual tool confirmation.
